# Post caption

Buying the phone was supposed to be the easy part.

It was out of stock online, so I opened Google Maps, found nearby electronics shops and started calling.

No answer. Wrong colour. No offers. “Come to the shop and see.”

I wanted a personal voice agent to make those first enquiries and tell me which shops were worth following up with.

That’s why I built Boomerang.

It calls the shops you choose and brings back their answers. Anything they don’t confirm stays unconfirmed. You decide who to call next and where to buy.
