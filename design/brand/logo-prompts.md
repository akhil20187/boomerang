# Boomerang logo prompts

Brand: **Boomerang**. You send it out, it talks to people (calls shops), and it comes back with answers.
Palette (from DESIGN.md / D82): black on white, one green accent (#16A34A) meaning "the shop has it".

Formula (from prompt research): concept + style + colours + constraints.
Shared constraints: flat vector logo, centred, plain white background, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockups, crisp geometric shapes, must still read at 32x32 px, 1:1.

1. **Return path** — Minimalist logo mark: a classic two-armed boomerang drawn as one thick rounded stroke, with its flight path curving out and back into a loop, suggesting "goes out and comes back". Swiss modernist style. Black mark with a small green (#16A34A) dot where the path returns. Flat vector, white background, no gradients, no shadows, no text, readable at 32x32 px, 1:1.
2. **Answer tick** — Minimalist logo mark: a boomerang whose two arms form a bold check mark, implying it returns with a confirmed answer. Geometric, rounded corners, single solid shape. Black with the shorter arm in green (#16A34A). Flat vector, white background, no gradients, no text, app-icon friendly, 1:1.
3. **Talks to people** — Minimalist logo mark: a boomerang shape whose inner curve forms a speech bubble tail, combining "boomerang" and "conversation". Bauhaus-inspired, thick uniform strokes. Solid black on white with a single green (#16A34A) accent. Flat vector, no gradients, no shadows, no text, readable at 32x32 px, 1:1.
4. **Wordmark** — Clean lowercase wordmark "boomerang" in a bold geometric sans-serif, tight letter spacing; the two "o" letters are joined into a looping return arrow. Black text, the loop in green (#16A34A). Flat vector, white background, horizontal 3:1 layout, no gradients, no tagline, perfectly spelled.
