# Sample service call briefs

Draft examples using the agreed [five-field brief and clarification flow](CALL_TASK_FLOW.md). Only the customer's one-line requests are known. Unspecified facts remain unknown; no provider has been selected or called. These examples do not establish that service-provider discovery or these tasks are supported by the live product.

## 1. Notary service: availability and office opening

**Customer request:** “I am looking someone who can provide notary service, is it available and is the shop open.”

### Task context

The customer wants a nearby notary service and needs to know whether the office is open and a notary is available when they visit. Location and intended visit time have not been supplied. No particular document or provider has been identified.

### Call purpose

Check whether this office offers notary services, whether a notary will be available and whether the office will be open at the requested time. Office opening and notary availability are separate facts.

### Possible expected outcome

Confirm whether the customer can approach this office for a notary at the intended time. If unavailable, obtain the next available time if the office can provide it. Return any visit instructions volunteered by the provider. Do not promise a document can be notarized without the provider confirming it handles that document.

### Who the agent represents

Gayatri, calling on behalf of the requesting customer. Use the customer's known first name if supplied; otherwise say “a customer”. This is a personal enquiry; no company has been specified.

### Call recipient details

A selected nearby notary office or service provider. Business name, location and usable phone number must come from the selected provider's record. None has been supplied in this example. Confirm the office identity and speak with whoever can confirm the notary's availability.

**First clarification:** “Which area or pincode should I look in?”

Then establish the intended visit time if it is still unclear. Ask about document type only if the customer wants confirmation for a particular document; a basic opening/availability enquiry does not require collecting document contents.

**Example call, once the customer confirms they want to visit now:**

1. “Hello, is this [office name]?”
2. “Hi, this is Gayatri. I'm checking for a customer. Do you provide notary services?”
3. If yes: “Is a notary available right now?”
4. If the office's opening status is still unclear: “Is the office open for a visit now?”
5. If unavailable: “When would a notary next be available?”
6. “Thanks. I'll pass that information on.”

Wait after each question. For a future visit, substitute the confirmed day/time for “right now”. Skip facts already answered. Report opening and notary availability separately; do not infer one from the other.

## 2. House painter: service fit and availability

**Customer request:** “Looking for a painter to paint my house.”

### Task context

The customer is looking for a painter for their home. Location, interior versus exterior work, extent of painting and preferred timing are not yet known. No budget, paint choice, house size or selected painter has been supplied.

### Call purpose

Find out whether the painter serves the customer's area and handles the kind of house painting required. Understand availability and the next step for discussing or assessing the work.

### Possible expected outcome

Identify a potentially suitable painter who covers the area and the requested work. Obtain an indication of availability and find out whether the painter needs further details or a site inspection before quoting. Return any estimate voluntarily given with its conditions. A firm price, booking or inspection appointment is not required for this initial enquiry.

### Who the agent represents

Gayatri, calling on behalf of the requesting customer. Use the customer's known first name if supplied; otherwise say “a customer”. No company or household details should be invented.

### Call recipient details

A selected house painter or painting contractor serving the customer's area. Use that provider's confirmed business/name and saved phone number. None has been selected in this example. Speak with the person who handles painting enquiries or estimates.

**First clarification:** “Which area is the house in?”

Next establish interior, exterior or both. Ask about the extent of work, house size, preferred timing or budget only as needed for the requested availability/estimate. If a particular painter has already been provided, a basic service enquiry may proceed while detailed quotation questions wait for clarification.

**Example call, after location and type of painting are known:**

1. “Hello, is this [painter or business name]?”
2. “Hi, this is Gayatri. I'm calling for a customer who needs [interior/exterior] house painting in [area]. Do you take that kind of work?”
3. If yes and area coverage is still unclear: “Do you work in [area]?”
4. If timing is supplied: “Would you be available around [preferred time]?” Otherwise: “When would you next be available?”
5. “Would you need to see the house before giving a quotation?”
6. “Great, thanks. I'll pass this on so the customer can get in touch about the details.”

Pause after each question and skip anything already answered. Do not book a visit, promise acceptance, disclose an exact address or agree to payment during this enquiry.

## Shared conversation preferences

Keep the opening short, follow the recipient's language, ask one question at a time and respond to the actual answers. Use a truthful short answer if asked about automation. The agent can call and return findings through the existing task/chat flow; it cannot send SMS or email. Preserve unknowns and partial answers in the summary.

The questions above illustrate what the assistant should clarify with a real customer. These are sample drafts, so they do not require the user to supply real locations or phone numbers now.
