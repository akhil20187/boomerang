## Four-step design drafted at your request

This section is an assistant-drafted synthesis of the discussion, not verbatim lock-sheet answers.

1. **Request:** customer asks their personal AI for an urgent product search. AI collects the exact product, location and relevant cards, researches online and identifies stores to contact.
2. **Approve:** customer sees the proposed task, billing and spending cap and authorises the calls.
3. **Execute and hand off:** AI attempts calls. Unanswered, refused or incomplete calls go to a human with requirements and previous attempts. If no worker accepts promptly, customer chooses whether to wait or cancel without charge.
4. **Return and choose:** AI combines confirmed results into a stock-and-price shortlist, separates conditional offers, marks unresolved stores unconfirmed, and lets the customer choose. A reservation can follow separately.
