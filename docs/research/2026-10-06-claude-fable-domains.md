# Boomerang domain suggestions from Claude Fable

**User selection, clarified:** Brand **Boomerang**, preferred domain **askboomerang.com** (D103, 6 October 2026). “Ask” is the domain prefix only. Availability and ownership are not verified.

6 October 2026. Requested by the user. Obtained using the installed Claude CLI with `--model fable`; the command completed successfully. Tools and MCP connections were disabled for this request. This was a naming brainstorm, not a domain availability check. No domain was purchased or changed.

## Recommendation

**askboomerang.com** — Claude's winner: describes the customer's action, fits calls today and human/expert work later, and is easy to say and spell.

| Rank | Domain | Reason or trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | askboomerang.com | Matches asking a question and getting an answer back |
| 2 | heyboomerang.com | Friendly and fits a product used inside chat |
| 3 | callboomerang.com | Connects calling the service with calls it makes; may constrain the broader identity |
| 4 | boomerang.ai | Compact alternative to .com; availability and cost unknown |
| 5 | boomerangit.com | Can become “just boomerang it”; joined spelling may be harder to scan |

## Other ideas returned

- boomerangback.com — Fits the return story but repeats what a boomerang already means.
- throwboomerang.com — Fits the metaphor but is clunky to say.
- boomeranghq.com — A practical corporate fallback with little product meaning.
- boomerangapp.com — Signals software but is generic; existing-brand overlap deliberately ignored for this exercise.
- myboomerang.com — Personal, though it may sound like an account page.
- boomerangcalls.com — Clear for today's service but narrow for future expert work.
- boomerang.so — Short alternative ending; customer familiarity would need testing.
- tossboomerang.com — Claude rejected it as careless-sounding and awkward for verified answers.

Claude's comments on earlier ideas: heyboomerang fits chat best; getboomerang and tryboomerang are generic startup conventions; doboomerang reads awkwardly. It did not explicitly rank sendboomerang or goboomerang.

## Limits and next step

These are Claude's editorial opinions, not measured customer preferences. No registrar, trademark register, prices or customer spelling tests were checked. Unverified price and trust speculation from its response is not treated as evidence here.
