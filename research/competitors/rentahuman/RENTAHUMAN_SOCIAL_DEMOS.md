## What their demonstrations suggest

**Interpretation:** the horizontal marketplace supplies people for many jobs, while narrow services make purchasing a particular result easier. AI coordinates requirements and people; humans provide the unavailable physical presence or firsthand judgment. The founder still performs parts of the operation in his sign-holding example.

For the phone-search idea, compare requirement transfer, worker instructions, follow-up handling and returned evidence. Do not treat a publicity stunt or the ability to post a task as proof that urgent Indian store calls will succeed.
