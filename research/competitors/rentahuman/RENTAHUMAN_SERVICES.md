# RentAHuman: categories, breadth and depth

Reviewed 3 October 2026. Sources: live official pages and Akhilesh’s screenshots captured 2 October. No task was purchased or completed. Social and video evidence is covered in [Social and demos](RENTAHUMAN_SOCIAL_DEMOS.md).

## Finding

RentAHuman spans physical tasks, digital work and human judgement. It combines a broad worker marketplace with more defined products such as line standing and software testing. It directly overlaps both parts of Human MCP: completing work an agent cannot finish and obtaining human judgement. Breadth of advertised work is clear; reliable delivery across every category is unverified.

### Line standing: a useful example of depth

The offer charges **$23.60 USD/hour including fees**, in New York City, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Toronto and Vancouver. Bookings require at least 12 hours’ notice and run 1–12 hours. Paying funds recruitment; confirmation requires a worker’s acceptance. No confirmation before the start produces a refund. Arrival photos, hourly updates and written handoff are specified. Purchases, identity representation and prohibited queue swapping are excluded. The page identifies its update date as **22 September 2026**. [Official offer](https://rentahuman.ai/services/line-standing).

Handyman, cleaning and errands links appear on the services page, but their detail pages could not be fetched. The assistant guide treats these as requests rather than instantly confirmed bookings. It explicitly says its named assistant guides do not establish partnerships or built-in connectors. [Assistant guide](https://rentahuman.ai/assistants).

## 4. What this means for Human MCP

**Interpretation:** the broad proposition is already occupied. “AI gathers requirements, hires a human and returns results” is not a sufficient distinction. RentAHuman also has concrete judgement/testing products, so framing it as exclusively physical labour would be inaccurate.

The phone-search version could still compete on a specific outcome: nearby Indian stock checks, exact variant, conditional card offers, evidence freshness, INR budget and transparent call billing. AI attempting calls before human fallback is a proposed execution difference; the inspected material does not establish whether RentAHuman already implements it. It is not a verified advantage.

The service catalogue inspected does not establish a vetted network delivering regulated professional opinions. Nor does it establish reliable immediate calling supply around 500037. Those are unknowns, not proven gaps.

Next research test: follow one relevant task from approval through worker acceptance and returned evidence. Keep category breadth, posted demand, worker profiles, accepted jobs and delivered results as separate measures.
