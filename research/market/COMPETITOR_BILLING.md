# Paying for human work: competitor comparison

Checked 3 October 2026 against public first-party terms and documentation. No paid tasks or billing tests performed.

## Answer

| Company | Customer pays for | How payment works | Is a desired outcome guaranteed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Humwork | Expert consultation time | Prepaid credits, billed in fixed time increments with a minimum charge; expert rate shown before consultation | No. Its terms do not guarantee the consultation outcome |
| RentAHuman | Agreed task/work; pricing depends on task or service | For ordinary escrow-backed tasks, funds are held, evidence reviewed, then payment explicitly released to the worker | Not universally. Completion criteria define the requested work; that is different from guaranteeing the customer's desired answer |

Sources: [Humwork terms, sections 2–4](https://humwork.ai/terms), [RentAHuman tool documentation](https://rentahuman.ai/docs).

## Humwork

One credit is approximately US$1, with tier differences. Consultations are charged in advance in fixed time increments and have a minimum charge. Ending early does not refund the unused part of an increment. Credit purchases are generally final, with discretionary consideration for platform errors or extraordinary cases. Expert payouts deduct a platform service fee; the inspected terms do not give a percentage. This is time-based consultation, not payment only for a successful solution. [Terms](https://humwork.ai/terms).

## RentAHuman

Task posting includes completion criteria and required evidence. Its documented ordinary task flow funds escrow—money held pending the work—then reviews delivery evidence and releases payment on the owner's explicit instruction. Eligible escrow can be cancelled or disputed; payment release is final. These mechanisms support payment for an agreed deliverable, not a universal rule of charging per verified store answer. Other service types may use hourly pricing. [Docs](https://rentahuman.ai/docs).

The terms specify a platform percentage disclosed before a transaction and possible payment-processing fees. They contain broad no-refund wording, alongside later provisions permitting refunds or withholding of task payments during errors/disputes and narrower language about non-refundable platform fees. Documentation also describes eligible escrow and wallet refunds. Consequently, do not interpret the public material as a blanket refund guarantee or blanket prohibition for every task. Exact applicable checkout and service conditions need confirmation. [Terms](https://rentahuman.ai/terms), [Docs](https://rentahuman.ai/docs).

## Our decision and proposal

**User decision:** “Pay for verified answers --> is better”. This replaces the earlier proposed customer billing by connected minutes and unanswered-call fees. Budget approval remains required. Worker compensation is a separate unresolved decision.

**Assistant recommendation, not a fully agreed pricing specification:** quote a price per completed store check before approval. A verified answer may be “no stock” or “price above your budget”; it need not be the answer the customer wants. Unanswered or incomplete calls remain unconfirmed. Ten requested checks with eight verified answers should be priced according to the eight completed units under this proposal, not the ten attempts.

**User evidence decision:** “caller notes should be good for now, but where possible recording available, should be auditable”. Notes are sufficient initially; attach a recording for audit when available. This does not independently prove accuracy. Required note fields, audit reviewer/access, unit price, definition of an acceptable negative answer, refund policy and dispute rules remain open. Caller effort and failed-attempt costs must be covered in worker pay and overall pricing even if the customer is not billed for them.
