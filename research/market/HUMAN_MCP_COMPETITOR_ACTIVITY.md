## Answer

**Both appear active. RentAHuman has stronger recent, dated public development evidence; Humwork has a live offer, hiring and an expert app, but its recent social posting frequency remains unknown.** Neither can be declared commercially successful from these checks. Both are marked Active in YC’s Spring 2026 directory. [Humwork YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/humwork), [RentAHuman YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rentahuman).

RentAHuman is expanding beyond physical errands into human testing of software. That brings it closer to the proposed expert/input handoff than its original physical-work positioning suggests. This is an interpretation of its current [Human QA offer](https://rentahuman.ai/qa).

## Humwork: what the footprint tells us

The offer is expert help when an AI agent gets stuck. The homepage publishes 3,000+ verified experts, an 83% resolution rate and under two minutes average first response. Those are **company claims** without an available measurement period, underlying task count or external audit in this research. An earlier company job advertisement claimed 2,800+ resolved questions and 87% success. These figures cannot be combined into a growth or decline trend without matching definitions and dates. [Homepage](https://humwork.ai/), [Company expert job listing](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/senior-full-stack-engineer-typescript-%E2%80%94-on-demand-expert-for-ai-agents-remote-flexible-at-humwork-yc-p26-4398779677).

Its public plugin repository contains setup instructions and human consultation tools. The retrieved page showed 28 commits, one star and one fork. These establish a public integration artifact, not usage of the hosted service. A hosted backend can change without updating this public repository. [Plugin](https://github.com/humworkai/humwork-mcp).

The expert app lists Orange AI Inc. as developer and founders@humwork.ai as support. The Humwork homepage also names Orange AI Inc. This confirms the legal-brand connection; it does not establish that old Orange AI product metrics belong to Humwork. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.humwork.expert), [Homepage](https://humwork.ai/).

**Assessment:** credible signs of an operating early company. Current social momentum and reliable expert availability in a chosen domain remain unverified.

## RentAHuman: what changed beyond the launch

The public line-waiting tool offers a specific task at $20/hour including fees in five named cities. Its instructions explicitly state payment starts recruitment and does not guarantee a worker has accepted. This is useful evidence of concrete product packaging and failure handling, but we did not test fulfillment. [Repository](https://github.com/rentahuman-ai/line-waiting-cli).

Human QA offers people testing an app or website journey, returning recordings, findings and steps to reproduce problems. It supports one-off and repeat tests with explicit budgets. The page also exposes mixed task reviews, including withdrawals and incomplete evidence. This is more informative than a landing page containing only positive testimonials, though these remain platform-published records. [Human QA](https://rentahuman.ai/qa).

Its homepage claims 750,000+ humans and $500,000+ paid to humans. **Worker payments are not company revenue or investment funding.** Registered people are not necessarily available or paid workers. YC’s founder biography separately claims 500,000 users and $20,000 monthly recurring revenue in two weeks; that is founder-supplied historical information, not an audited current revenue figure. [Homepage](https://rentahuman.ai/), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rentahuman).

Founder Alexander Liteplo’s public QA post reports $1 million in free AI API credits split between OpenAI and Anthropic. These are **usage credits, not cash funding**, and the post does not establish either company invested equity. His reported two-to-three-hour QA turnaround is also self-reported. [Founder post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alexander-liteplo_update-as-part-of-our-acceptance-to-yc-we-activity-7495132476814790656-BelT).

**Assessment:** clear signs of continuing product work in September, with multiple concrete services and investor confirmation. Repeat purchasing, margins and task quality remain unknown.

## Funding confidence and next step

YC participation is confirmed for both. Accelerator membership is not a verified total-raised figure, latest round announcement or valuation. Directory estimates for Humwork and RentAHuman were inconsistent or lacked accessible primary support, so they are not promoted to fact here. No public amount found is different from no investment received.

For the sprint, these companies justify studying execution rather than dismissing the category as an abandoned idea. They also reduce the distinction of a broad “human MCP” marketplace. Next, compare one real task against an existing service: time to acceptance, useful evidence returned, total cost, failure handling and whether the buyer would repeat. That test provides more decision value than follower counts.
